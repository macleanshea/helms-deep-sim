Model sheet of a single-seat ground-attack jet aircraft of original design: straight broad wings set low, two engine pods mounted high on the rear fuselage, twin tail fins, a bubble canopy well forward, a blunt nose, stores pylons under the wings. Matt grey-green paint, worn panel lines, exhaust staining. Shown as a scale model for a museum display: the whole aircraft in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view slightly above, on its own, in level flight attitude with the undercarriage up. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no stand, no crew, no smoke, no text, no markings or insignia, no border. The same gritty painted realism and the same dusty olive-drab and grey palette as the vehicle in the reference painting.
