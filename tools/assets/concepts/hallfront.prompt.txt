Model sheet of the stone front of a great hall that is built into a cliff, of the same pale weathered ashlar as the walls in the reference picture. Just the front wall, as one slab a few metres thick, about one and a half times as wide as it is tall. Below, on a stepped plinth, five tall round-headed arches between heavy buttress piers, each opening deep and black inside. Above a moulded string course, a gallery of ten small round-headed arched windows, deep and black inside. A carved cornice of corbels under the eaves and a steep dark slate roof sloping back. At each end of the roof ridge a carved stone horse's head. Old, massive and plain, chipped edges, damp streaks under the sills. The front stands entirely on its own: no rock, no ground, no figures, no banners, no doors. Seen straight on from the front and very slightly above, the whole front in frame with a little margin. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no text, no border.
