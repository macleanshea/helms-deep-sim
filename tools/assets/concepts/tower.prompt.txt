Model sheet of the same siege tower as in the reference painting: a tall siege tower of rough dark timber on a wheeled base with eight solid wooden wheels, square in plan and about three times as tall as it is wide, leaning in slightly toward the top. Its front and sides are boarded with gaps between the boards and hung with overlapping wet raw hides; cross-braced corner posts show at the back, which is open with ladders inside from floor to floor. At the top a boarded fighting platform with a wooden parapet, and from the top storey a plank boarding bridge lowered straight forward, level, held by two chains. A ragged dark banner on a short pole above. No figures, no crew. The whole tower is in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view, slightly above, on its own. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no smoke, no text, no border. Same gritty painted realism and the same palette of dark weathered timber, rust and raw hide as the reference.
