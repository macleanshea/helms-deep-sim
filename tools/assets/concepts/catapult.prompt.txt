Model sheet of the same siege catapult as in the reference painting: a torsion catapult of rough dark timber on four solid iron-bound wooden wheels. A heavy rectangular frame of squared beams; across its middle a thick twisted rope skein; from the skein a single stout throwing arm ending in an iron-bound wooden cup, drawn back and down to the rear in the loaded position with a rough stone ball in the cup; at the rear a windlass with handspikes; at the front two uprights carrying a padded crossbar that the arm strikes. Iron bands, rivets, pegs and rope. No figures, no crew. The whole machine is in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view, slightly above, on its own. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no smoke, no text, no border. Same gritty painted realism and the same palette of dark weathered timber, rust and raw hide as the reference.
