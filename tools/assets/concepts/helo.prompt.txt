Model sheet of a two-seat attack helicopter of original design: a narrow fuselage with stepped tandem cockpits, short stub wings carrying rocket pods, a chin gun under the nose, twin engines on the shoulders, a long tail boom with a small tail rotor, and one four-bladed main rotor on a mast above, its blades straight and level. Matt olive-drab paint, worn panel lines, exhaust staining. Shown as a scale model for a museum display: the whole aircraft in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view slightly above, on its own, in level flight attitude with the undercarriage up. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no stand, no crew, no smoke, no text, no markings or insignia, no border. The same gritty painted realism and the same dusty olive-drab and grey palette as the vehicle in the reference painting.
