Model sheet of a four-engined turboprop military transport aircraft of original design: a high straight wing with four propeller engines, a fat round fuselage with an upswept rear and a loading ramp, a tall single tail fin, a short blunt nose with a row of cockpit windows. Matt olive-grey paint, worn panel lines, oil streaks behind the engines. The propellers are shown as still four-bladed propellers. Shown as a scale model for a museum display: the whole aircraft in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view slightly above, on its own, in level flight attitude with the undercarriage up. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no stand, no crew, no smoke, no text, no markings or insignia, no border. The same gritty painted realism and the same dusty olive-drab and grey palette as the vehicle in the reference painting.
