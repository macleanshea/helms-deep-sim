Model sheet of a large single-rotor transport helicopter of original design: a long deep fuselage with a row of square windows and a wide sliding door, a rounded glazed nose, two engines side by side on the roof, a slim tail boom with a small tail rotor, fixed wheels on short struts, and one five-bladed main rotor on a mast above the roof, its blades straight and level. Matt olive-drab paint, worn panel lines, exhaust staining. Shown as a scale model for a museum display: the whole aircraft in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view slightly above, on its own, in level flight attitude . Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no stand, no crew, no smoke, no text, no markings or insignia, no border. The same gritty painted realism and the same dusty olive-drab and grey palette as the vehicle in the reference painting.
