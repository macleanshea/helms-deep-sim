Model sheet of a single tall fortress tower built of the same pale weathered ashlar stone as the walls in the reference picture: an eight-sided shaft about seven times as tall as it is wide, tapering gently upward, rising from a broad splayed stone plinth. Four deep stepped buttresses climb two thirds of its height. Narrow arrow loops in a rising spiral, two string courses, a few small round-headed windows near the top. Under the top a corbelled gallery with a stone parapet runs all the way round. Above it an open crown: eight slender stone piers joined by pointed arches round an open chamber, and over them a low eight-sided stone spire with a small finial. Massive, plain and old, with chipped edges, darker damp streaks and a little moss low down. The tower stands entirely on its own: no rock, no ground, no other walls, no banners, no figures. The whole tower is in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter view slightly above its mid height. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no text, no border.
