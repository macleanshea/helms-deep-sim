Model sheet of the same wheeled timber machine as in the reference painting, as a museum reconstruction of a medieval engine: a low timber carriage on four solid iron-bound wooden wheels, with a steep shed roof of rough planks covered in overlapping raw hides nailed down in panels, open at both ends. Under the roof a massive tree trunk hangs level on chains from the ridge beam; its front end, capped in black iron forged as a wolf's head, juts out well beyond the front of the roof. Rough dark timber, rusted iron bands and rivets, rope lashings, stained wet hides. No figures, no crew. The whole machine is in frame with a little margin, seen from a three-quarter front view, slightly above, on its own. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio lighting, no cast shadow, no ground, no smoke, no text, no border. Same gritty painted realism and the same palette of dark weathered timber, rust and raw hide as the reference.
